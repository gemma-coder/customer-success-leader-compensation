Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions CS leaders ask most about their compensation.

What is the average Chief Customer Officer (CCO) salary?

CCO base salaries in the US range from $150K to $450K depending on company stage, with total cash compensation of $345K to $720K at $25M to $100M ARR companies. Median equity value is $2.4M per the Pavilion 2025 report, though this varies widely by stage. In the UK and Europe, CCO base runs £180K to £250K with 30% to 40% variable on top.

How much equity should a VP of Customer Success receive at a Series B company?

At Series B companies ($20-100M raised), VP-level executives receive a median 0.33% total equity per Pave data. A competitive VP CS package at this stage typically includes 0.3% to 0.5% equity with 4-year vesting and a 1-year cliff. For CCO roles at the same stage, expect 0.5% to 1%+ equity.

What is the typical variable pay percentage for a VP of Customer Success?

VP of Customer Success variable pay runs 20% to 30% of base salary on average, with the median at 30% and mode at 20% per Pave 2023 private company data. Leaders who own renewals, expansion or NRR targets typically earn 15% to 30% more in total cash per Melo Associates.

How does CS leader compensation differ between the US, UK, Europe and Canada?

US CS leaders earn meaningfully more than UK, European and Canadian peers. A VP CS US median base is around $240K, while London base runs £170K to £200K, Dublin €200K to €225K, Amsterdam €200K to €230K, and Toronto CA$245K to CA$315K per Pave benchmarks. The US premium is roughly 25% to 40% depending on the specific market compared.

Has CS leader compensation gone up or down since 2023?

The picture is mixed. C-level OTE dropped 19% year-over-year per Pavilion's 2025 report, while SVP/EVP is the only tier where pay grew (+4%). Base salary floors have risen at Director and VP level — it is harder to underpay a mid-level CS leader now than it was two years ago. The wild outlier CCO packages of 2023 have moderated, and equity participation has dropped from 85% (2023) to 77% (2025).

What is the gender pay gap in Customer Success leadership?

At Director of Customer Success level, the global gender pay gap is 26.4%, with men earning meaningfully more than women per Customer Success Collective's 2024 report. The gap widens at senior levels — Heads of CS show up to 45.8% lower pay for women vs male counterparts. In Europe, the gap narrowed from 16% to 11% year-over-year per Customer Success Snack 2025.
